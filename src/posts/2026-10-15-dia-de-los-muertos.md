**Día de los Muertos in Las Cruces: Arte, Música y Cultura** is a free community event downtown on **November 1**.

Getting your ofrenda ready? We carry catrinas, painted calaveras, marigold accents and Día de los Muertos merchandise year-round. Stop by 130 S Main St before the celebration.
