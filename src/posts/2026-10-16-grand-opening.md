After our farmers market days, **3 Brothers Shop LLC finally has a location!** Our doors are open at **130 S Main St** in downtown Las Cruces.

Come browse rustic furniture, hand-painted pottery, longhorns, Mexican figurines and our full line of Día de los Muertos merchandise. Gracias to everyone who supported us along the way. We can't wait to see you in the shop.
