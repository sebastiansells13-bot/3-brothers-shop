# Editing the 3 Brothers Shop website

You can update the site yourself, with no code.

## 1. Log in

1. Go to **https://app.pagescms.org/edit/sebastiansells13-bot/3-brothers-shop**
2. Sign in with your GitHub account. Your web developer adds you to the project first.

## 2. What you can edit

- **Shop Info & Hours**: phone, email, address, hours and social links. These show up
  in the footer, on the Visit page and on the map.
- **Home Page**: the red banner at the top, the hero photo, and the intro text.
- **What We Carry**: the product categories, each with a photo and a short description.
- **Photo Gallery**: the Instagram-style photo grid. Add, remove or reorder photos.
- **About Page**: the shop's story and photo.
- **News & Events**: announcements, farmers market days, new arrivals. Each post has a
  title, short summary, date, type, an image or flyer, and body text.

## 3. Making a change

1. Click the section you want to edit (for example "News & Events")
2. Click an item to edit it, or **Add** to create a new one
3. Fill in the fields. Required fields are marked.
4. Click **Save**

Saving publishes the site automatically. Changes usually show up in 1–3 minutes, so
refresh the page after a few minutes if you don't see yours yet.

## 4. Photos

- Use the image field's **upload** button. Don't paste a link.
- Phone photos are fine. The site compresses them automatically when it publishes.
- Instagram flyers (square or tall) work well for News posts. Use tall photos for the
  home page hero.

## 5. Getting help

If something looks wrong after you save, contact your developer. Please don't edit
files directly on GitHub.
